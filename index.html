                How Wheel Be Fine works

                Driving can be nerve-wracking for new teen drivers. Wheel Be Fine gives you a safe place to practice first.

                1

                Small steps

                Each drive will start in an empty parking lot. As the stages progress, one new challenge will be added at a time: a quiet road, stop signs, pedestrians, and other cars. Taking it a step at a time can make driving more manageable.

                2

                Calm tools

                A guided breathing exercise before each session, a pause button always available, and a quick check-in before and after each drive can help ground you before practicing and will track your progress.

                3

                Smooth controls

                Steering and pedals build up gradually, so you learn to brake early and turn smoothly instead of jerking the wheel.

                4

                Go at your own pace

                Each drive has an associated difficulty level. Score 60 or more on a drive to complete it and unlock the next one. Finish all six to earn a certificate.

                Disclaimer

                Wheel Be Fine is practice, not a replacement for real driving lessons. Always learn with a licensed adult or instructor, and follow your state's rules for learner's permits.
